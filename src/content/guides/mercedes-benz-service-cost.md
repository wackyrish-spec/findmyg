**Short answer:** independent garages in Dubai publish a Mercedes Service A (the smaller of the two scheduled services) from about AED 250 to 1,250 for most models, and about AED 1,360 for the larger S-Class and GLE/GLS. A G-Class costs AED 1,764. Service B starts from about AED 350. Front brake pads on a C- or E-Class, fitted, are published at AED 400 to 650 per axle, and an AC re-gas from AED 150. Mercedes dealers in the UAE don't publish service prices, so to compare, get the dealer's quote in writing and set it next to these.

**Why one Service A costs five times another:** the gap is mostly in what's included. A Service A should follow the Mercedes schedule: the approved oil (MB 229.5 or 229.51 spec, depending on the engine), an oil filter, the checks on the schedule, and a reset of the service indicator. Ask which oil and filter are used, whether the cabin filter is included, and whether VAT is included. A low "from" price on an offer page is a starting point, not a quote for your car.

**Service A or Service B?** Your instrument cluster tells you which one is due. Service B is the larger one (it adds items such as the cabin filter and brake fluid, depending on the model and year), and it costs more.

## Dealer or independent?

- **Under warranty or a service contract:** stay with the dealer for warranty work, and check your terms before servicing elsewhere.
- **Out of warranty:** an independent with Mercedes diagnostic software (XENTRY or equivalent, to read faults and reset the service) does routine work for much less. Ask for an itemised quote.
- **Common Mercedes jobs in Dubai heat:** air suspension (Airmatic) on the S-Class, E-Class and GLE/GLS, engine mounts, and AC components. Get two quotes for any of them.

## FAQ

**How much is a Mercedes service in Dubai?**
Independent garages publish a Mercedes Service A from about AED 250 to 1,250 for most models, about AED 1,360 for the S-Class and GLE/GLS, and AED 1,764 for the G-Class, including VAT (garage price pages, Nov 2026). Mercedes dealers in the UAE don't publish service prices.

**What's the difference between Mercedes Service A and Service B?**
Service A is the smaller scheduled service, built around an oil and filter change and checks. Service B adds more items, such as the cabin filter and brake fluid depending on the model and year, so it costs more. The car tells you which one is due.

**Does servicing at an independent void my Mercedes warranty in the UAE?**
It depends on your warranty terms, so check them, or ask the dealer in writing, before using an independent while the car is under warranty. Out of warranty, it's your choice.
