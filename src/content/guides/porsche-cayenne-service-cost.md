**What moves the price:** the engine (a V6 Cayenne takes less oil than a Turbo), genuine Porsche parts versus OE-equivalent, which filters are included, and whether the garage uses Porsche diagnostics (PIWIS) to reset the service indicator and read stored faults. A cheap price that skips the scan isn't a bargain on a Cayenne.

## Oil service

The dealer also lists an oil service on its own: starting prices of AED 1,098 for the 958 and AED 1,220 for the 9Y0, including VAT ([dealer Q4 2026 promo](https://dealer.porsche.com/ae/dubai/en-GB/Service-Promo-Q4_2026)). Independents usually fold this into their minor service. PRO.CTO lists an oil and filter change for Porsches at AED 210, which looks like labour only, so check whether oil is included before you compare.

## Brakes

The dealer's promo shows Cayenne brake work at starting prices of AED 1,738 (958) and AED 2,182 (9Y0), including VAT. The promo doesn't say exactly what's included, so ask before you compare. AMF publishes Porsche brake pads, fitted on one axle, at AED 1,890 to 4,725 including VAT, with discs and sensors extra. The range is wide because ceramic and Turbo brakes cost far more than standard ones.

Britfix, in Al Quoz 4, publishes Cayenne front pads with wear sensors, fitted, at AED 800 to 1,400 per axle, VAT not stated ([Britfix](https://britfix.ae/services/brake-pads), Nov 2026).

Front pads alone aren't a big job. Discs, sensors and brake fluid are what turn a quote into a bill, so ask for each one to be listed separately.

## Dealer or independent: how to decide

- **Still under warranty or Porsche Approved?** Stay with the dealer for anything warranty-related, and check your terms before using an independent for servicing.
- **Out of warranty:** a Porsche specialist with PIWIS, genuine or OE parts and an itemised quote does the same routine work for much less. Ask what's included, ask for the old parts back, and ask whether the price includes VAT.
- **Big jobs** (air suspension, transfer case, coolant pipes on the older V8s): get two quotes. These are where specialist knowledge saves the most, and published prices don't exist yet. Owner reports on KnowsMyCar will fill that gap.

## FAQ

**How much is a Porsche Cayenne service in Dubai?**
At the dealer, a minor service starts from AED 1,590 (958) or AED 1,960 (9Y0) including VAT, per its Q4 2026 promo. Independent Porsche specialists publish minor services from about AED 700 to 1,575, depending on the model and what's included.

**Is it cheaper to service a Cayenne at an independent garage in Dubai?**
For routine servicing, published prices suggest it usually is: the lowest independent prices are about half the dealer's starting price. Choose a Porsche specialist with Porsche diagnostics, ask for an itemised quote, and check your warranty terms if the car is still covered.

**Does servicing at an independent void my Porsche warranty in the UAE?**
It depends on your warranty terms, so check them, or ask the dealer in writing, before using an independent while the car is under warranty. Out of warranty, it's your choice.
