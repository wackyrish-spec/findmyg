**Short answer:** in November 2026 the Porsche dealer in Al Quoz advertises a minor service at starting prices of AED 1,535 to 2,265 including VAT, depending on the model. Independent Porsche specialists in Dubai publish the same job at about AED 640 to 1,500, depending on the model and engine. Across the models, the independent price works out at about 40% to 70% of the dealer's starting price.

I look after a Cayenne myself, and the one thing I'd tell any Porsche owner out of warranty is this: you have a choice. Here are the published numbers, by model, with where each one came from.

Sources: [dealer Q4 2026 service promo](https://dealer.porsche.com/ae/dubai/en-GB/Service-Promo-Q4_2026) and [Deutsche Technik's price list](https://dtservicecentre.com/porsche-service-cost-dubai/), both checked 6 to 8 Nov 2026. Deutsche Technik's prices don't say whether VAT is included, and its model groups don't split by generation.

**Other independents:** AMF Auto Services (PorscheGarage.ae) publishes Porsche minor services at AED 840 to 1,575 including VAT ([price list](https://porschegarage.ae/)), and PRO.CTO lists a package (oil, filter and checks) from AED 699 ([price list](https://procto.ae/porsche-price.html)), both as of 6 Nov 2026.

**What moves the price:** engine size and oil capacity, genuine Porsche or OE-equivalent parts, which filters are included, and whether the garage uses Porsche diagnostics (PIWIS) to read faults and reset the service. Ask for an itemised quote, and ask whether VAT is included.

## Oil service and brakes at the dealer

The same dealer promo lists an oil service at starting prices of AED 1,055 (Macan) to 1,485 (911), and brake work at starting prices of AED 1,738 to 2,228 depending on the model, all including VAT. The brake price doesn't say what's included, so ask before comparing.

For comparison, AMF publishes brake pads fitted on one axle at AED 1,890 to 4,725 including VAT, with discs and sensors extra ([price list](https://porschegarage.ae/), 6 Nov 2026). The range is wide because ceramic and Turbo brakes cost far more.

## Buying a used Porsche?

A pre-purchase inspection at a Porsche specialist costs far less than the problems it can find. One owner reported paying about AED 900 for a 911 inspection at Project 11 ([Reddit, Sep 2023](https://www.reddit.com/r/DubaiPetrolHeads/comments/16ba73o/porsche_pre_purchase_inspection/)). That's a single report, so ask for a current price.

## Dealer or independent?

- **Under warranty or Porsche Approved:** stay with the dealer for anything warranty-related, and check your terms before servicing elsewhere.
- **Out of warranty:** a Porsche specialist with PIWIS, genuine or OE parts and an itemised quote does the routine work for much less.
- **Big jobs:** get two quotes. Air suspension, PDK and engine work are where specialist knowledge saves the most, and published prices for them don't exist yet.

## FAQ

**How much is a Porsche service in Dubai?**
At the dealer, a minor service has starting prices of AED 1,535 (Macan) to 2,265 (911) including VAT, per its Q4 2026 promo. Independent Porsche specialists publish minor services at about AED 640 to 1,575, depending on the model.

**Is it cheaper to service a Porsche at an independent in Dubai?**
For routine servicing, published prices say yes: independents' published prices are about 40% to 70% of the dealer's starting price, depending on the model. Choose a Porsche specialist with Porsche diagnostics and ask for an itemised quote.

**Does servicing at an independent void my Porsche warranty in the UAE?**
It depends on your warranty terms, so check them, or ask the dealer in writing, before using an independent while the car is under warranty. Out of warranty, it's your choice.
