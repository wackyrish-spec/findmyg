**Short answer:** an independent Land Rover specialist in Dubai publishes a fixed-price Range Rover minor service at AED 982 to 1,270 including VAT, depending on the model and year. Land Rover dealers don't publish service prices. The one dealer figure we have is an owner's quote of "almost AED 4,000" for a 2024 Defender minor service. One market guide puts dealer servicing at 30 to 50% more than an independent for a 2019 Range Rover.

## Defender: what an owner was quoted at the dealer

An owner of a 2024 Defender P300 shared their quote from the authorised dealer: a minor service of "almost AED 4,000" and a major service of AED 8,950, not including brake pads ([Reddit, Aug 2026](https://www.reddit.com/r/DubaiPetrolHeads/comments/1vg37sc/land_rover_defender_p300_2024_major_service/)). That's one owner's quote, not a price list, but it's the clearest dealer figure published. A newer Defender may still be under warranty, so check your terms before you go independent.

## Air suspension: the big one

Most Range Rovers in Dubai eventually need air suspension work: air springs, the compressor or the valve block. Published independent prices for luxury SUVs run AED 1,500 to 5,000 per air spring and AED 2,500 to 8,000 for a compressor ([ArabWheels, Jul 2026](https://www.arabwheels.ae/blog/air-suspension-in-uae-how-it-works-types-and-common-issues/)). Those are general figures, not Range Rover quotes. A specialist with Land Rover diagnostics will tell you which part has actually failed, and that's where most of the saving comes from.

## Buying a used Range Rover?

Have a Land Rover specialist inspect it first. Ask them to check the air suspension, coolant leaks, timing chains on the V8s and stored electrical faults. The cost of an inspection is small next to any one of those repairs. See [garages owners trust for pre-purchase inspections](/dubai/problem/buying-used/).

## FAQ

**How much is a Range Rover service in Dubai?**
An independent specialist publishes fixed-price minor services at AED 982 to 1,270 including VAT, depending on the model (A2B Garage, Nov 2026). Dealers don't publish prices; one market guide puts dealer servicing at 30 to 50% more than an independent.

**How much does a Land Rover Defender service cost at the dealer in Dubai?**
One owner of a 2024 Defender P300 was quoted almost AED 4,000 for a minor service and AED 8,950 for a major service at the authorised dealer (Aug 2026). That's a single quote, so ask for an itemised one for your car.

**Does servicing at an independent void my Land Rover warranty in the UAE?**
It depends on your warranty terms, so check them, or ask the dealer in writing, before using an independent while the car is under warranty. Out of warranty, it's your choice.
