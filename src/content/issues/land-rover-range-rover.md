The L405 Range Rover (2013 to 2021) is one of the most common luxury SUVs in Dubai, and one of the most expensive to keep once the warranty ends. Here is what goes wrong most, with the UAE prices we could find.

**About prices.** Unlike most models, the Range Rover has published UAE prices, from CarSwitch and ArabWheels. They're ranges, not quotes. A UAE guide puts dealer prices about 30 to 50% above independents. If you've had one of these done in Dubai, add what you paid in your review.

## Car sitting low, "suspension fault" message
**Trade name:** air suspension (air springs, compressor, valve block, height sensors).
**What you notice:** one corner sags overnight, a suspension warning, the compressor running for a long time, or a hard, crashy ride.
**How common:** the most-cited Range Rover problem in every source we read. Heat is hard on the rubber air springs.
**UAE cost:** air spring AED 1,500 to 5,000. Compressor AED 2,500 to 8,000 (CarSwitch gives AED 3,500 to 6,000 for the L405). Valve block AED 1,500 to 4,000. Height sensor AED 500 to 2,000. The ArabWheels figures cover all makes with air suspension.

## Rattle on start-up
**Trade name:** timing chain and tensioners.
**UAE cost:** about AED 8,000 to 14,000 for a V6 timing chain service (CarSwitch). Don't ignore a start-up rattle. A failed chain can wreck the engine.

## Screen freezing or rebooting
**Trade name:** InControl Touch Pro / infotainment faults.
**How common:** reported most on 2013 and 2014 cars.
**UAE cost:** AED 4,000 to 7,000 for a screen (CarSwitch). A software update sometimes fixes it, so ask first.

## Hesitation or judder when pulling away (2013 to 2014)
**Trade name:** low-speed transmission judder.
**Cost abroad:** major transmission repair $4,000 to $6,500 in the US. The source sells car reports, so treat this one as a guide only.

## Coolant level dropping
**Trade name:** coolant leaks (pipes, water pump).
**How common:** "occasional" (CarSwitch).
**Cost abroad:** water pump $800 to $1,500 in the US.

## Wet carpets after rain or a wash (2017 cars)
**Trade name:** windscreen seal leak. Land Rover issued a service bulletin to re-bond windscreens.

## Recalls to check by VIN
Early L405s had recalls for **door latches** that might not hold (2013 to 2015) and **front suspension arms** (2015). Recall work is free at the dealer.

## Wear items, for budgeting
- Front pads and discs: AED 2,500 to 4,000.
- Minor service: AED 2,500 to 4,000. Major service: AED 6,000 to 12,000 at an independent.

## What to ask the garage
1. "Which part of the air suspension has failed: the spring, the compressor, the valve block or a sensor?" A leaking spring can burn out the compressor, so ask if both need doing.
2. "Genuine, OEM or aftermarket air springs, and what warranty?"
3. "Can you show me the fault codes before replacing anything?"
4. For a screen fault: "Have you tried a software update first?"
5. Get a written quote, with parts and labour split, and compare it with the ranges above.
