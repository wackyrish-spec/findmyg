The Patrol is the UAE's own 4x4, and Dubai owners keep them for years, often with plenty of desert driving. Below are the problems that owners' forums, UAE and international buying guides, and recalls report most for the Y62 (2010 on) and the Y61 Super Safari (TB48 4.8 petrol). We list only what a source backs up, and we link every source.

**How we show cost.** Where a UAE source publishes a price, we show it in AED. Otherwise we show the size of the job. **Small:** a part or a software update, done in a morning. **Moderate:** a day's work or a costly part. **Major:** a big assembly or the engine comes apart, or the parts alone are very expensive. **Free:** recall work at the dealer.

## Patrol Y62 (2010 on, 5.6 V8, 7-speed automatic)

### Harsh shifts, slow engagement or a shudder between 2nd and 3rd
**Trade name:** 7-speed automatic (RE7R01A) valve body.
**How common:** a UAE used-car guide says early Y62s have a "known reputation for valve body issues", mainly 2010 to 2014 cars. An Australian review says "enough owners" report odd shift behaviour, especially when towing in heat, and suggests a fluid change about every 30,000 km, even though Nissan calls it a lifetime fill.
**UAE cost:** AED 5,000 to 12,000 for a valve body repair, depending on severity (CarSwitch).

### The car flexes oddly, rides stiff, or the mounts look perished (HBMC cars)
**Trade name:** Hydraulic Body Motion Control (HBMC) shocks and mounts, fitted on higher trims in place of anti-roll bars.
**How common:** owners on the Nissan Patrol forum report perished mount rubbers and front shocks going stiff. One expects HBMC shocks to become "a reasonably regular change over" as the cars age. Some owners convert to conventional suspension with anti-roll bars.
**Cost:** Moderate to Major, parts-heavy.

### Clunks over bumps and vague steering after desert driving
**Trade name:** worn suspension bushes. The UAE guide calls this "a common result of repeated desert impact". It also flags uneven tyre wear from dune and rock hits.
**Cost:** Moderate.

### ABS, stability or tyre-pressure warnings that come and go
**Trade name:** wheel speed and TPMS sensor faults. The UAE guide says these "sometimes give intermittent faults" on desert-used cars.

### Brakes and tyres wearing fast
**Trade name:** front pads, discs and tyres. A South African buyer's guide says the Patrol's weight causes "accelerated wear and tear" on brakes and tyres.
**Cost:** Small to Moderate.

### Recalls to check by VIN
- **Engine control module** (UAE, July 2010): 1,250 new 2010 Patrols were recalled because a circuit could fail and stop the engine.
- **Fuel pressure sensor** (Australia): a batch of Y62s was checked for a sensor that could loosen and leak fuel. GCC cars may differ, so ask the dealer.
- The new Y63 Patrol (2025) had a UAE recall for torque-transfer software (1,661 cars).

Recall work is free at the dealer.

## Patrol Y61 Super Safari (TB48 4.8 petrol)

GCC Super Safaris are petrol automatics. Most Australian Y61 horror stories are about the ZD30 diesel or manual gearboxes, so they don't apply here.

**Trade name:** cooling system (radiator, hoses, fan). A Y61 buyer's guide warns that "all Patrol engines can have overheating issues" and says to check the radiator, hoses and fan.

## Buying a used one? What to check
- **Service history**, and when the gearbox fluid was last changed. On an early Y62, ask whether the valve body has been done.
- **HBMC:** is it original, or converted to conventional suspension? Look for leaks at the shocks and lines, and perished mounts.
- **Desert use:** sand in the underbody, bent skid plates, damaged sills, uneven tyre wear and clunks over bumps.
- **Coolant:** clean, and at the right level. Milky coolant on a Y61 is a walk-away sign.
- **Modifications:** lifts, tunes and big tyres all add wear.
- **Recalls:** ask the dealer to check the VIN.

Book a [pre-purchase inspection](/dubai/problem/buying-used/) with a garage that knows Patrols. See the [Patrol garages owners rate](/dubai/nissan/patrol/).

## What to ask the garage
1. On a Y62 with harsh shifts: "Is it the valve body, or will a fluid change and software update fix it?"
2. On an HBMC car: "Can you pressure-check the HBMC system, and what would a conversion cost against a repair?"
3. "Can you show me the fault codes before replacing any sensors?"
4. "Is there an open recall on my VIN?"
5. Get a written quote, with parts and labour split, before agreeing.
