The Land Cruiser's name is built on reliability, so this page is short on purpose. Even so, a few jobs come up again and again in owners' forums and the trade press, and the LC300 has had a big UAE recall. We list only what a source backs up, and we link every source.

> **LC300 recall, October 2026:** 29,774 Land Cruiser 300s (2021 to 2024) are recalled in the UAE for engine and gearbox faults. **Check if your car is included:** ask your dealer with your VIN; recall work is free. Source: [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/auto/toyota-recalls-nearly-29800-land-cruiser-300s-in-uae-over-engine-and-transmission-issues-1.500696874).

## LC300 (2021 on, 3.5 twin-turbo V6, 10-speed automatic)

### Recalls in the UAE: check your VIN first
In October 2026, Al-Futtaim Toyota and the UAE Ministry of Economy and Tourism recalled 29,774 Land Cruiser 300s built in Japan from 2021 to 2024, in three campaigns:
- **Engine bearings** (6,571 cars, 2023 to 2024): machining debris can damage the crankshaft bearings. Signs are knocking, rough running, hard starting or stalling. The fix is an inspection and, where needed, a free replacement engine.
- **Engine software** (7,453 cars): power may not match spec at full throttle when the cooling fan runs. The fix is a free software update.
- **Gearbox** (15,750 cars): some gears can over-rev, damaging the transmission or losing drive, and fluid can leak. The fix is a free inspection and reprogramming.

**Cost:** Free. Most LC300s are still under warranty, so other faults should go to the dealer for now.

## LC200 (2007 to 2021)

### Coolant on the ground or at the top of the radiator
**Trade name:** radiator top-tank crack.
**How common:** the most-reported repair in an IH8MUD owners' poll (44% of 162 votes). The forum says it "appears to happen more in older 200's (2008-2010)".
**Cost:** Moderate.

### Coolant level dropping, pink crust at the front of the engine (V8)
**Trade name:** water pump leak. 19% of owners in the same poll had replaced one. One owner was "shocked at how early these pump issues are showing up", and a leak can damage the fan bracket bearing.
**Cost:** Moderate.

### Car won't crank, or the starter clicks
**Trade name:** starter motor (solenoid) failure. The IH8MUD FAQ says "a few 200 owners have had their starter fail abruptly".
**Cost:** Moderate.

### KDSS bolts seized or the valve rusted (cars with KDSS)
**Trade name:** Kinetic Dynamic Suspension System valve and bolts. Owners say cars up to 2017 used hex bolts that rust and snap, and 2018 on cars use standard bolts.
**Cost:** Moderate.
[Tell us what you paid](/check-a-quote/?make=Toyota&model=Land%20Cruiser&job=other&note=LC200%20KDSS%20valve%20or%20bolts)

### Air won't switch between fresh and recirculate
**Trade name:** HVAC recirculation flap hinge. Owners say catching it early saves a much bigger repair.
**Cost:** Small if caught early.

### Clunk when pulling away
**Trade name:** dry driveshaft universal joints. The trade press calls it "common" on 200s that go off-road. Greasing them is often the fix.
**Cost:** Small.

## Buying a used one? What to check
- **Service history**, and any coolant top-ups. Look for crust around the water pump and the radiator top tank.
- **Off-road and dune use:** sand underneath, bent skid plates, clunks on take-off.
- **KDSS or AHC suspension:** even ride height, no leaks, bolts not seized.
- **Dashboard and interior:** heat damage on the dash top.
- **Recalls:** on an LC300, ask the dealer to confirm all three 2026 campaigns are done.
- **Modifications:** lifts, tunes and big tyres add wear.

Book a [pre-purchase inspection](/dubai/problem/buying-used/) with a garage that knows Land Cruisers. See the [Land Cruiser garages owners rate](/dubai/toyota/land-cruiser/). Lexus LX570 and LX600 owners: same mechanicals, so most of this page applies.

## What to ask the garage
1. "Can you pressure-test the cooling system before replacing the pump or radiator?"
2. On a KDSS car: "Can you free the bolts without snapping them, and what if they snap?"
3. On an LC300: "Have all the recall campaigns been done on my VIN?"
4. "Genuine Toyota or aftermarket parts, and what warranty on the work?"
5. Get a written quote, with parts and labour split, before agreeing.
