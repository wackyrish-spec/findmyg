*Last updated: {{updated}}*

KnowsMyCar is a free guide to independent garages in Dubai. It's run by {{operator}}, an individual based in Dubai ("we"). This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do. Questions: {{emailPrivacy}}.

**The short version**
- You can browse without an account. We don't use advertising trackers and we don't sell data.
- If you write a review, we publish your review, your display name and the cars you list. Everything else (email, WhatsApp number, receipts) stays private.
- You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time.

**What we collect**

| What | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Email address, and your name from Google if you sign in with Google | You sign in to write a review | To run your account, stop fake and duplicate reviews, and contact you about your review |
| Your review: car, job, price, ratings, text, photos | You write a review | To publish it. It's public once approved. |
| Display name and the cars you list | You write a review | Shown with your review so readers know who's speaking. You choose the display name. |
| Receipt or invoice (optional) | You upload one | Only to check the visit happened. Never published. Deleted 12 months after the review is approved. |
| WhatsApp number (optional) | You add it on the review form | Only to contact you if your review is disputed. Never published, never given to garages. |
| Garage suggestions and "tell me when my car is covered" requests | You fill in a form | To add garages, and to email you once, when your car is covered |
| Technical data: IP address, device and browser type, pages visited | Any visit | To keep the site secure, stop spam and fake reviews, and count visits. Our analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) don't use cookies or build profiles. |
| Your saved car | You pick "What do you drive?" | Stored only in your own browser (local storage), not on our servers |

**Review photos.** Photos you add to a review are checked with the review before anything is published. Please cover or blur number plates and people's faces; if you don't, we will, or we won't publish the photo. We strip location and camera data (EXIF) from every photo when it's uploaded. You can ask us to remove a photo at any time.

**Our legal basis.** We process your data because you asked us to (your account and review), because you agreed (optional receipt, WhatsApp number, update emails), and because we have a legitimate need to keep reviews genuine and the site secure.

**Who we share it with**
- **The public:** your published review, display name and cars.
- **Service providers** who host and run the site for us: Supabase (accounts, reviews, files), Cloudflare (hosting, security, analytics), Google (if you choose Google sign-in), our email provider. Their servers may be outside the UAE, for example in the EU or the US. We only use providers that protect data to a standard at least as good as UAE law requires.
- **Garages:** never your email, number, receipts or identity. A garage sees only what the public sees.
- **Authorities:** only if UAE law, a court or the police require it.

We don't sell or rent personal data, and we don't use it for advertising.

**How long we keep it**
- Account and reviews: while your account is open. If you delete your account, we remove your name from your reviews or delete them, your choice.
- Receipts: 12 months after approval, then deleted.
- Moderation records (what was reviewed, decided and why): 3 years, because we may need them if a review is disputed.
- Technical logs: up to 90 days.

**Your rights**
You can ask us to: tell you what data we hold and give you a copy; correct it; delete it; stop using it for something you agreed to; or object to how we use it. Email {{emailPrivacy}} from the address on your account. We'll reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the UAE Data Office.

**Security.** Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited to the people who moderate the site, and receipts are stored in a private store that is never public.

**Children.** KnowsMyCar is for adults. Don't create an account if you're under 18.

**Changes.** If we change this page in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top and tell account holders by email.
