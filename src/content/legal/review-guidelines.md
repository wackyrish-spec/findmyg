---
title: "How to write a review on KnowsMyCar"
---

KnowsMyCar is built on honest reviews from owners. Your review helps the next owner of your car find a garage that knows it. Here's what we ask.

**Write about your own visit.** Only review a garage you used yourself, for your own car (or a car you were responsible for), in the last 3 years. One review per garage every 6 months. If you go back, update your review.

**Stick to what happened.** Say what car, what job, what you were quoted and what you paid, how long it took, and whether the problem was fixed. "Quoted AED 2,400, paid AED 3,100, and the extra wasn't explained" helps people. "These guys are crooks" doesn't, and we won't publish it.

**Your opinion is welcome. Keep it clearly yours.** "I felt rushed" or "I wouldn't go back for gearbox work" is fine. Don't accuse anyone of a crime (fraud, theft, scam) as a fact. If you think something illegal happened, report it to Dubai Police or the Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism consumer protection, not in a review.

**No insults, no names.** No swearing, insults or comments about anyone's nationality, religion, gender or looks. Don't name or describe individual staff in a negative review. You can thank a mechanic by first name in a positive one.

**No private information.** Don't post phone numbers, number plates, invoices with personal details, or photos of people. Receipts you upload privately are only seen by our moderators and are never published.

**Be independent.** Don't review a garage you own, work for, are related to, or compete with. Don't accept money, discounts or free work for a review. If a garage offered you something to leave a review, tell us in the review form. Don't threaten a garage with a bad review to get a refund.

**Photos.** Only photos you took. Your car, the work, the workshop. No people's faces, no number plates (blur them).

**What happens next.** Every review is read by a person before it goes live, usually within 2 days. We may ask you a question, or ask you to change wording. We don't change what you said without asking. We publish negative reviews that follow these rules, and we never take money to remove or hide a review.

**If you're a garage.** You can reply to any review, and ask us to look again at one you think is false. See [Report a review or reply as a garage](/right-of-reply/).

By submitting a review you confirm it is true to the best of your knowledge and is your own experience. You stay responsible for what you write. Making false statements about a business can be a crime in the UAE.
