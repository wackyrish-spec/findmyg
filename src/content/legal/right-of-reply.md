title: "Report a review, or reply as a garage"
---

We want every garage on KnowsMyCar to be treated fairly. If something on our site about you is wrong, tell us. We don't charge for any of this, and we never take money to remove, hide or reorder reviews.

**Garages: reply to a review**
Every garage can post one public reply under any owner review. Your reply goes through the same quick check as reviews (no insults, no customer's personal details such as their name, plate or invoice). We publish it within 2 working days.

**Ask us to look again at a review**
Use this if you believe a review is false, isn't from a real customer, breaks our [review guidelines](/review-guidelines/), or shares private information. Tell us which review, what's wrong, and send anything that helps (for example, "we have no record of this car"). We'll:
1. Confirm we got your request within 2 working days.
2. Hide the review while we check, if it accuses anyone of a crime or shares private details.
3. Ask the reviewer for their side and, where it helps, a receipt. They have 14 days to reply.
4. Decide and tell you both, usually within 21 days.

We remove reviews that break our guidelines or that the reviewer can't support. We don't remove a review only because it's negative.

**Correct your garage's details**
Wrong phone, hours, address, services or specialties? Tell us and we'll fix facts within 7 days.

**Remove your garage**
If you'd rather not be listed, tell us. We'll remove your contact details and specialty labels. Owner reviews may stay, because they belong to the owners who wrote them, unless they break our guidelines.

**Anyone: report a review**
Every review has a "Report" link. Use it for anything that looks fake, abusive or private.
