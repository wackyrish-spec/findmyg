**Short answer.** Most BMWs since about 2010 use the ZF 8-speed automatic (8HP). Its common issues are leaks, not failures: the mechatronic sleeve where the wiring plug enters the gearbox, and the O-rings on the cooler lines. BMW calls the fluid "lifetime", but ZF's own guidance is to change it at about 8 years or 80,000 to 120,000 km.

## Common faults
- **Mechatronic sleeve leak:** drips at the gearbox connector, then gearbox warnings or no drive if fluid reaches the electronics. **Job:** a sleeve swap, plus a new pan and filter, since the pan comes off anyway. The sleeve breaks easily if forced, so it needs a careful hand. AC condensation from the back of the car can look like a sleeve leak.
- **Cooler line O-rings:** fluid leaking from above the pan on the driver's side. Heat cycling deforms them, and the cooler works hardest in heavy traffic. **Job:** an O-ring swap.
- **Old fluid:** if the fluid is burnt or full of debris, a simple drain and fill isn't enough. Ask what they'll do.

Stop-start traffic and heat work the cooler hard, so check for leaks and change the fluid on time.

## Affected
The 8HP45 (2009 on) and 8HP50 (2015 on): the F10 5 Series, F01 7 Series, E70/F15 X5, F16 X6, F30 3 Series and most BMWs since about 2010. Older 6-speed cars (E90, E60, to 2008) have a different sleeve with its own BMW bulletin.

## What to ask the garage
1. "Is it the sleeve, the cooler lines or the pan?"
2. "Will you replace the pan and filter and refill with the correct ZF fluid?"
3. "What does the old fluid look like?"
