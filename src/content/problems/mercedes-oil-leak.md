**Short answer.** The M276 V6 and M278 V8 (used in the E-Class, S-Class, ML, GL and GLE from about 2011) commonly seep oil at the upper timing covers, the camshaft magnet O-rings and the oil filter housing. Mercedes has a bulletin on it: find the exact source, using tracer dye if needed, then reseal.

## Signs
- Oil weeping at the front of the engine, on one or both sides.
- Oil on the cam sensors and their wiring. It can wick down the wires.
- Oil reaching the alternator.
- A burning smell after a drive.

## Where it leaks
- **Upper timing covers:** sealed with liquid sealant, not a gasket.
- **Camshaft magnet O-rings.**
- **Oil filter housing and oil cooler.**
- **Water pump and thermostat seals, and the oil pan:** the Mercedes bulletin lists these too.

## How big is the job?
It's a reseal. The left timing cover is the easier side. On the M276, the right side needs the intake manifold off. While it's apart, owners often have the chain tensioners and cam sensors done, and on the M278 the plastic turbo coolant pipes. We have no published Dubai prices yet.
