**Short answer.** Early Y62 Patrols, mostly 2010 to 2014 cars, have a known reputation for valve body problems in the 7-speed automatic (RE7R01A). A UAE guide puts a valve body repair at AED 5,000 to 12,000, depending on severity.

**Got a quote?** [Check it here](/check-a-quote/?make=Nissan&model=Patrol&job=other&note=Gearbox) before you agree to it.

## Signs
- Harsh or late shifts.
- A slow engagement into Drive or Reverse.
- A shudder between 2nd and 3rd gear.

## Prevention
Nissan calls the gearbox oil a lifetime fill. An Australian used-car review still suggests changing it about every 30,000 km, especially on cars that tow or see the desert. Ask your garage what fluid they use and whether they flush or drain and fill.

## What to ask the garage
1. "Can you read the gearbox codes and adaptation values before quoting?"
2. "Is this the valve body, or the whole gearbox?"
3. "Repair, exchange or new unit, and what warranty?"
4. "Is there an open recall on my VIN?" (Y62 and Y63 recalls are listed on our issues page.)
