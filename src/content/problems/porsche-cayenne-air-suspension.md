**Short answer.** Cayennes with air suspension (many 958s from 2011 to 2018, and the 9Y0 from 2019) can develop leaks in the struts or valve block, or a tired compressor. It's a parts-heavy job: moderate to major, depending on how many corners and parts are involved. Leaks are worth catching early, before the compressor wears out working against them.

**Got a quote?** [Check it here](/check-a-quote/?make=Porsche&model=Cayenne&job=other&note=Air%20suspension) before you agree to it.

## Signs
- The car sits low after being parked.
- The compressor runs a lot.
- A chassis or suspension warning comes on.
- The ride feels uneven from one side to the other.

## What to ask the garage
1. "Can you show me the fault codes before quoting?"
2. "Is it the strut, the valve block or the compressor? If a strut leaks, has the compressor suffered too?"
3. "Genuine, OEM or aftermarket parts, and what warranty on the work?"
4. "Is there an open recall on my VIN?"
