**Short answer.** On the L405 Range Rover (2013 to 2021), air suspension is the problem every source we read cites most. A leaking spring makes the compressor run longer, and that can burn the compressor out, so one fault often turns into two.

**Got a quote?** [Check it here](/check-a-quote/?make=Land%20Rover&model=Range%20Rover&job=other&note=Air%20suspension) before you agree to it.

## Signs
- One corner sits low after the car has been parked overnight.
- A "suspension fault" message on the dash.
- The compressor runs for a long time, or you hear it often.
- The ride turns hard and crashy.

## What usually fails, and UAE prices
These are price ranges published by UAE sources, not quotes. CarSwitch notes that dealers run about 30 to 50% above independents.

The ArabWheels figures cover every make with air suspension, not just Range Rover.

## What to ask the garage
1. "Which part has failed: the spring, the compressor, the valve block or a sensor? Can you show me the fault codes?"
2. "If the spring is leaking, has it damaged the compressor too?"
3. "Genuine, OEM or aftermarket springs, and what warranty?"
4. Get a written quote with parts and labour split, and compare it with the ranges above.
