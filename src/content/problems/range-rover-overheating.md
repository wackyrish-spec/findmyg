**Short answer.** On the L405 Range Rover (2013 to 2021), a dropping coolant level usually comes from a leaking coolant pipe or the water pump. CarSwitch calls these leaks "occasional". It's a moderate job: a day's work or a costly part. It's worth fixing early in Dubai heat, because running low on coolant leads to overheating, and that is far more expensive.

**Got a quote?** [Check it here](/check-a-quote/?make=Land%20Rover&model=Range%20Rover&job=other&note=Coolant%20leak) before you agree to it.

## Signs
- You keep topping up the coolant.
- There's a sweet smell after driving.
- Pink or white crust shows on pipes or around the engine.
- The temperature gauge climbs in traffic or on long climbs.

## What to ask the garage
1. "Can you pressure-test the system and show me where it's leaking?"
2. "Is it a pipe, the water pump or the expansion tank?"
3. "Genuine, OEM or aftermarket parts, and what warranty?"
4. Get a written quote with parts and labour split.
